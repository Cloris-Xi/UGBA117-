Privacy & data use

This is a course-project prototype. Here's exactly what happens to your data — no accounts, no hidden tracking.

Assignment text & team info

What you type into the assignment box and team form stays in your browser. When you click "Build a Team Plan," it's sent to a small server function to generate the task plan (using an AI model where one is configured, or a free local rule-based planner otherwise) — it isn't stored on any server or database afterward. Uploaded images and PDFs are sent to the AI model the same way — for that one request, then discarded, never saved to disk. DOCX and TXT files are read and converted to text directly in your browser and never leave your device unless you click "Build a Team Plan" or "Fill team from upload."

Saved locally, on your device only

Your current plan is saved in this browser's local storage so it's still here next time you visit. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or sync it (see below). Clearing your browser data will remove it.

Google Sheets, Calendar & Gmail

Connecting Google is optional and only happens if you click "Connect Google." We request the minimum access needed — creating/editing spreadsheets, creating calendar events, and sending email on your behalf — nothing else (not your existing emails, contacts, or files). The access token is kept in memory only for that browser session and is never saved to disk. Nothing is sent to Google unless you click "Save to Google Sheets," "Add to Google Calendar," or "Send reminder email." If you fill in teammates' email addresses, "Add to Google Calendar" sends them a calendar invite and "Send reminder email" sends them an email with the task plan — both only when you click the button, and only do this with your teammates' knowledge.

CSV export

Downloads a file directly to your device. It doesn't pass through any server.

Automatic email reminders (Step 4)

This is one of two features that does save data outside your browser — it has to, since it needs to run without you present. Turning it on stores your confirmed deadline date and your teammates' emails on a small database so a scheduled job can email them as the deadline approaches. Turning reminders off deletes this record.

Account sync (Step 5)

Also optional. If you click "Save to my account," your whole plan (assignment text, team, tasks) is stored on the same database, tied to your Google account's email — so you can get it back by connecting the same Google account on another device. This overwrites whatever was previously saved for that account. Nothing is synced automatically; it only happens when you click "Save to my account" or "Load from my account."

Team-shared plan ("Working with a team?")

If you create or join a shared plan, your team's plan (assignment text, team info, tasks) is stored on the same database under a share code, and updates are pushed there automatically as you make changes (so teammates can stay in sync) — no Google login required for this one. Anyone who has the code or link can view and edit that plan, the same way anyone with a Google Docs link can if you share it — so only share the code with your actual team.

What we don't do

No ads, no third-party trackers, no selling data, no account or password required.
